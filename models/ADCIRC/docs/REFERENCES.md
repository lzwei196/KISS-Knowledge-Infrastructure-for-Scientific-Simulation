## Key Papers

2. **Westerink, J.J., Luettich, R.A., Feyen, J.C., Atkinson, J.H., Dawson, C., Roberts, H.J., Powell, M.D., Dunion, J.P., Kubatko, E.J., and Pourtaheri, H.** (2008). 4. **Bunya, S., Dietrich, J.C., Westerink, J.J., et al.** (2010). A High-Resolution Coupled Riverine Flow, Tide, Wind, Wind Wave, and Storm Surge Model for Southern Louisiana and Mississippi. Part I: Model Development and Validation. *Monthly Weather Review*, 138(2), 345–377.
