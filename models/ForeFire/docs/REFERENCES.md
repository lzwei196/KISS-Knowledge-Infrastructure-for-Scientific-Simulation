## Key Papers

- **Rothermel (1972)**: Rothermel, R.C. *A mathematical model for predicting fire spread in wildland fuels*. USDA Forest Service Research Paper INT-115.

- **Balbi et al. (2020)**: Balbi, J.-H., Morandini, F., Silvani, X., Filippi, J.-B., & Rinieri, F. (2009). A physical model for wildland fires. *Combustion and Flame*, 156(12), 2217–2230. Updated Balbi2020 formulation in ForeFire.

- **Andrews (2018)**: Andrews, P.L. (2018). *The Rothermel surface fire spread model and associated developments: A comprehensive explanation*. RMRS-GTR-371. USDA Forest Service.

- **Filippi et al. (2009)**: Filippi, J.-B., Bosseur, F., Mari, C., Lac, C., Le Moigne, P., Cuenot, B., Veynante, D., Cariolle, D., & Balbi, J.-H. (2009). Coupled atmosphere-wildland fire modelling. *Journal of Advances in Modeling Earth Systems*, 1(4).
