## Related Software

- **GenCase**: Case generator (XML → initial particle distribution)
- **PartVTK/PartVTKOut**: bi4 → VTK converter for ParaView visualization
- **MeasureTool**: Interpolation at measurement points (bi4 → CSV)
- **IsoSurface**: Free-surface extraction from particle data
- **Project Chrono**: Rigid body dynamics coupling (optional)
- **MoorDyn+**: Mooring line dynamics coupling (optional)
