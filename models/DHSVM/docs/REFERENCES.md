# DHSVM References

## Official Repository
- **Source Code**: https://github.com/pnnl/DHSVM-PNNL
- **Version**: 3.2 (current build)
- **Maintained by**: Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL)

## Key Publications

1. **Wigmosta, M.S., Vail, L.W., and Lettenmaier, D.P.** (1994). A distributed hydrology-vegetation model for complex terrain. *Water Resources Research*, 30(6), 1665-1679. https://doi.org/10.1029/94WR00436

2. **Wigmosta, M.S., Nijssen, B., Storck, P., and Lettenmaier, D.P.** (2002). The Distributed Hydrology Soil Vegetation Model. In V.P. Singh and D.K. Frevert (Eds.), *Mathematical Models of Small Watershed Hydrology and Applications*, Chapter 2, pp. 7-42. Water Resources Publications.

3. **Sun, N., Wigmosta, M., Zhou, T., Lundquist, J., Dickerson-Lange, S., and Cristea, N.** (2018). Evaluating the functionality and streamflow impacts of explicitly modelling forest-snow interactions and canopy gaps in a distributed hydrologic model. *Hydrological Processes*, 32(13), 2128-2140. https://doi.org/10.1002/hyp.13150
