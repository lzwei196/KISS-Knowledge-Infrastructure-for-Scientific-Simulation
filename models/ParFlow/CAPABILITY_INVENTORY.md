## Top 5 Gaps (Highest Priority)

### Gap 1: Wells and Pumping (6.1, 6.2) -- IMPACT: HIGH

ParFlow has a full well package (`well.c`, `well.h`, `well_package.c`) supporting extraction and injection wells with prescribed flux or pressure. This is critical for:
- Groundwater management scenarios
- Irrigation water supply studies
- Managed aquifer recharge

**Effort**: ~400 lines (tool to configure well locations, rates, and cycles).

### Gap 2: Adaptive Timestepping (7.4) -- IMPACT: HIGH

ParFlow supports growth-factor adaptive timestepping (`select_time_step.c`) where dt grows during dry periods and shrinks during rainfall events. The current KI uses Constant timestep only, which is either too slow (small dt during dry spells) or risks convergence failure (too large dt during sharp wetting fronts).

**Effort**: ~50 lines (add TimeStep.Type = "Growth" option to script generator with GrowthFactor, MinStep, MaxStep).

### Gap 3: Spinup Automation (10.5) -- IMPACT: HIGH

The KI documents spinup strategy (10-100 years of recycled forcing) but has no tool to automate it. Spinup requires:
- Chaining restart files across multi-year cycles
- Monitoring water balance convergence (dS/dt approaching zero)
- CLM restart file management

**Effort**: ~300 lines (spinup loop tool with convergence monitoring).

### Gap 4: Multi-Region Indicator Geometry (3.6) -- IMPACT: MEDIUM-HIGH

The script generator creates a single "domain" geometry with uniform properties. Real basins have multiple soil units (clay, sand, loam) that need separate indicator regions with distinct K, porosity, and van Genuchten parameters. `build_subsurface_properties.py` generates spatially distributed arrays, but the generated run script does not consume them.

**Effort**: ~200 lines (extend script generator to produce indicator-based geometry from subsurface tool output).

### Gap 5: Downstream Coupling Tools (10.2, 10.3) -- IMPACT: MEDIUM

ParFlow-to-DSSAT and ParFlow-to-LDNDC coupling tools are listed as planned but not built. These are needed for the full HydroCraft multi-model chain where ParFlow provides soil moisture to crop and biogeochemistry models.

**Effort**: ~400 lines total (2 tools, analogous to existing `parflow_to_cama.py`).
