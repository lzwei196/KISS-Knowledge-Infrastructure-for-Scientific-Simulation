## Key Papers

- Anderson, J. L. (2001). An Ensemble Adjustment Kalman Filter for Data Assimilation. *Monthly Weather Review*, 129(12), 2884-2903. doi:10.1175/1520-0493(2001)129<2884:AEAKFF>2.0.CO;2

- Anderson, J. L. (2003). A Local Least Squares Framework for Ensemble Filtering. *Monthly Weather Review*, 131(4), 634-642. doi:10.1175/1520-0493(2003)131<0634:ALLSFF>2.0.CO;2

- Anderson, J. L. (2009). Spatially and temporally varying adaptive covariance inflation for ensemble filters. *Tellus A*, 61(1), 72-83. doi:10.1111/j.1600-0870.2008.00361.x

- Anderson, J. L., Hoar, T., Raeder, K., Liu, H., Collins, N., Torn, R., & Avellano, A. (2009). The Data Assimilation Research Testbed: A Community Facility. *Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society*, 90(9), 1283-1296. doi:10.1175/2009BAMS2618.1

- El Gharamti, M. (2018). Enhanced Adaptive Inflation Algorithm for Ensemble Filters. *Monthly Weather Review*, 146(2), 623-640. doi:10.1175/MWR-D-17-0187.1

- Anderson, J. L. (2019). A Non-Gaussian Ensemble Filter Update for Data Assimilation. *Monthly Weather Review*, 147(12), 4229-4252. doi:10.1175/MWR-D-19-0077.1

- Anderson, J. L. (2022). A Quantile-Conserving Ensemble Filter Framework. Part I: Updating an Observed Variable. *Monthly Weather Review*, 150(5), 1061-1074. doi:10.1175/MWR-D-21-0229.1
